# BioFlow design principles

Healthy biology meets intelligent technology: light canvas, clean white surfaces, restrained teal interaction, organic edge decoration, precise evidence and calm typography. The reference supplies composition and visual language; its illustrative health values are not application data.

Prioritize selected-person context, understandable evidence and progressive disclosure. Do not invent scores, medical improvement, interpretations, recommendations, activity, sleep or nutrition measurements. Unknown data is visibly unknown.

Extend existing primitives through variants and composition. Do not introduce a second design framework, screenshot backgrounds, heavy botanical wallpaper, continuous decorative motion, or isolated page themes. Clinical status always uses text as well as styling.
