# Lifestyle Plans and Progress

## Goal
Help users follow practical, non-medication habits around diet, activity, sleep and routine measurements, with choice and control.

## Plan inputs
User goals, preferences, mobility limitations, known conditions, contraindications, available time, clinician guidance where recorded, and relevant verified measurements.

## Plan lifecycle
Suggested → reviewed by user → accepted → scheduled → check-in → adapted → paused/completed.

## Rules
- Never promise biomarker improvement.
- Avoid unsafe blanket advice for pregnancy, kidney disease, eating disorders, severe symptoms or other sensitive contexts.
- Never recommend changing prescriptions.
- Clearly distinguish general wellness guidance from medical treatment.
- Reward consistency and reflection, not shame or streak punishment.

## Metrics
Adherence chosen by user, voluntary check-ins, activity minutes when supported, sleep patterns, and observed biomarker changes with appropriate time windows.

## Acceptance
Users can dismiss goals without penalty and see which personal information informed a recommendation.
