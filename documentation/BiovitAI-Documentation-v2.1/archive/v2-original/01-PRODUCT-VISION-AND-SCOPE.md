# Product Vision and Scope

## Vision
Make personal and family health information understandable over time without pretending to replace clinicians. The product should be approachable for older adults and powerful for caregivers.

## Primary outcomes
- Users can locate, understand and compare their medical records.
- Users can see trends from reports, devices and manual entries with provenance.
- Users can identify which organs or conditions have relevant evidence and which are not assessed.
- Users can ask natural-language questions and receive evidence-linked answers.
- Users can follow safe, achievable lifestyle goals with non-coercive progress feedback.
- Family access is explicit, scoped, revocable and independently authenticated.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Autonomous diagnosis or prescribing.
- Medication initiation, cessation or dose changes.
- Claims of whole-body health scores.
- Emergency monitoring, continuous clinical surveillance or guaranteed medical outcomes.
- Automatic access to a relative's information based solely on knowing an email address.

## Key product principles
Evidence over speculation; source traceability; verified over extracted-but-unreviewed; privacy by default; clarity over gamification; human control over automated actions; one shared health data/analytics layer across chat and screens.

## Success metrics
Verified report ingestion completion, extraction correction rate, time to answer a report question, grounded-answer rate, successful authorized family sharing, accessibility task completion, weekly voluntary engagement, and user-reported understanding.

## Launch constraints
Determine intended medical use and relevant regulatory obligations for target markets with qualified counsel. Clinical interpretation and safety policies require clinician review.
