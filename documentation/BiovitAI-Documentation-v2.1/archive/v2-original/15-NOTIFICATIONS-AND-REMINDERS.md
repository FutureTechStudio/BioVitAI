# Notifications and Reminders

## Categories
Upload completed, verification needed, new analysis available, device disconnected, planned check-in, invitation pending, permission changed.

## Controls
Per-category opt-in/out, quiet hours, timezone, delivery channels, caregiver preferences and frequency caps.

## Privacy
Lock-screen and email notifications should avoid sensitive clinical specifics by default. A family member never receives another person's details without active consent.

## Safety
Not an emergency alerting service. Do not promise detection of urgent conditions through delayed uploads or asynchronous analytics.

## Acceptance
Permission revocation immediately suppresses future profile-specific notifications.
