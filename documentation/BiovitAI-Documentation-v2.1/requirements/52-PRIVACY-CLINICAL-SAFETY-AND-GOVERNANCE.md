# 52 — Privacy, clinical safety and governance
Status: Required pre-launch controls; not legal advice · Owner: Privacy + Clinical Safety

## Health data handling
Data minimization and purpose limitation; encrypted data at rest/in transit; least-privilege access; scoped consent across family profiles; private-by-default reports/goals; short-lived signed downloads; access logs; revoke sharing quickly, including caches and derived context. Upload identity mismatch → quarantine. Audit prompt/tool context access. Never share PHI with Community or another profile by default.

## Clinical safety model
Deterministic comparability and aggregation rules reviewed by qualified laboratory/clinical reviewer. Preserve source observation immutability, comparator and original units/ranges. Unknown data is unknown, not a green score. Never infer diagnosis, medication changes or emergency response from a general AI coach. Maintain escalation pathways, product disclaimers, documented exclusions, issue reporting, and update/remediation process.

## Wellness safety
Micro-actions reviewed against contraindications and feasibility; do not recommend through pain/illness without context. Personalization must be user-directed and reversible. No coercive gamification, shame wording or punitive lapse resets. Device gaps distinguished from actual non-adherence. No unsupported causal claim between biometric changes and habits.

## Jurisdiction launch checklist
Document target countries, hosting location, cross-border transfers, privacy notices, consent, minors/family delegation, records retention, clinical claims and applicable device/health-data regulation **with qualified local counsel**. Abu Dhabi/UAE alignment is a target hypothesis; this document does not certify regulatory compliance or assume access to Sahatna, IFHAS, Malaffi or other APIs.

## Community boundary
Public identities separate; no default health auto-share; credible source labels; moderation, report, appeal, removal and incident response. Treat community posts, partner content and uploaded reports as untrusted inputs for prompt-injection purposes.

## Governance artifacts
Threat model, data-flow map, model/provider register and residency assessment, action-catalogue approvals, clinical-rules registry, AI evaluation logs with de-identified goldens, incident/runbooks, DPIA/PIA, consent matrix, approved disclaimers, moderation playbook, go/no-go signoff.
